The problem

A threshold alarm tells you the box got warm.
It can't tell you what that did to the product.

Fixed-limit alarm

  • Fires once a value crosses a line
  • Forgets the exposure when temperature recovers
  • Treats a 10-second blip like a 10-minute excursion
  • Samples at one fixed rate, wasting battery

ColdSense exposure model

  • Scores severity × duration × repetition
  • Remembers every excursion — condition only moves down
  • Shocks and rough handling spend the same risk budget
  • Speeds up sampling when risk rises, relaxes when safe
Capabilities

Six engines, one small box.

Cumulative Risk Engine

Every second spent outside 2–8 °C adds weighted risk. Longer, hotter and repeated excursions cost progressively more.

Remaining Safe-Life Estimator

Turns accumulated risk into a single product-condition percentage — 95 %, 80 %, 55 % — anyone can read at a glance.

Adaptive Sampling

20 s when calm, 10 s when elevated, 3 s when critical. Fewer wake-ups on a battery, full detail when it matters.

Edge Processing

The ESP32 decides on its own. If Wi-Fi drops it keeps scoring, buffers readings and back-fills the cloud on reconnect.

Shock & Handling Detection

MPU6050 polled at 50 Hz catches millisecond impacts between samples and triggers an immediate reading.

Decision Engine

Converts condition into an action: Continue, Monitor, Prioritize Delivery, Inspection Required or High Risk.

Pipeline

From raw sensor reading to a decision in five steps.

  1. 01

    Sense

    DHT11 temperature & humidity, MPU6050 shock & tilt.

  2. 02

    Compute

    Exposure risk accumulates on the ESP32 itself.

  3. 03

    Estimate

    Condition % = 100 − accumulated risk.

  4. 04

    Adapt

    Risk level sets the next sampling interval.

  5. 05

    Report

    OLED on the box, Firebase to this dashboard.

Risk model · runs on the ESP32
excursion  = max(T − 8, 2 − T, 0)
risk      += excursion × (Δt / 60) × 0.9 × (1 + 0.25 × (repeats − 1))
risk      += max(shock − 1.8, 0) × 1.5
condition  = max(0, 100 − risk)

Each °C·minute outside the band costs 0.9 points, every repeated excursion costs 25 % more, and each g above the shock limit costs 1.5 points. All constants are reported live by the device.
